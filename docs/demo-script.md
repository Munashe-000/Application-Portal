# Demo Script

## Recommended Demo Flow

1. Open `index.html`.
2. Show the home panel and explain that the design intentionally matches a simple university portal style.
3. Open `Applicant Portal` and demonstrate the eligibility gate.
4. Show the consent capture and mandatory document list.
5. Open `Staff Dashboard` and switch between roles to demonstrate restricted visibility.
6. Point out the audit trail and shortlist gating rules.
7. Return to `Overview` and explain the ranked master scorecard.
8. End on `POPIA Controls` and use `docs/proposal.md` for the deeper technical discussion.
