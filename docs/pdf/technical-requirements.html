Requirements

1. Purpose and Scope

The system will support an annual application programme that includes applicant registration, eligibility screening, document submission, multi-stage review, comparative scoring, interview scoring, final decisions, and applicant communications.

2. In Scope

  • Applicant-facing portal with required forms and document upload
  • Multi-role staff dashboard with workflow-gated access
  • Essay, academic, document, shortlisting, interview, and final decision stages
  • Master scorecard with ranking and export capability
  • Audit logging, encryption, data export, and deletion workflows

3. Out of Scope for Initial Demo

  • Production authentication integration
  • Live payment processing
  • External student information system integration
  • Bulk data migration from legacy platforms

4. Functional Requirements

4.1 Applicant Portal

IDRequirement
FR-01The system shall present three initial eligibility questions before the main application form.
FR-02If any eligibility answer is negative, the system shall stop the application flow and display a clear ineligibility message.
FR-03The system shall reserve an application reference number even for ineligible submissions for reporting purposes.
FR-04The system shall capture explicit consent before personal information is submitted.
FR-05The system shall require upload of mandatory supporting documents before final submission.
FR-06The system shall prevent duplicate active submissions for the same applicant within the same cycle.

4.2 Review Workflow

IDRequirement
FR-07Essay reviewers shall score essays without visibility into academic results or peer reviewer scores.
FR-08Academic reviewers shall capture subject marks and the system shall calculate the average automatically.
FR-09Document checkers shall record required document completeness and issue notes.
FR-10Screeners shall access aggregated score and checklist views only after prior stages are complete.
FR-11Interview admins shall capture manual interview scores and notes for shortlisted applicants.
FR-12Final decision makers shall assign Approved, Rejected, or Standby outcomes.

5. Workflow and Data Rules

5.1 Workflow Enforcement

  • The system shall enforce ordered stage progression.
  • The system shall prevent shortlisting until essay review, academic scoring, and document verification are complete.
  • The system shall prevent interview scoring unless the applicant has been shortlisted.
  • The system shall prevent final decision completion until interview scoring is complete where required.

5.2 Scorecard Rules

  • Teaching experience scoring shall be rule-driven, for example 3+ years = 5 points, 1-2 years = 3 points, under 1 year = 1 point.
  • TEFL and degree presence shall contribute binary points as configured.
  • Essay and interview scores shall be pulled from role-owned scoring records.
  • The system shall calculate total score automatically and rank applicants by score.
  • The system shall support filtering and spreadsheet export of scorecard data.

6. Role and Permission Requirements

IDRequirement
PR-01Every user shall be assigned one or more roles and all access shall be policy-controlled.
PR-02The API shall enforce role permissions independently of the user interface.
PR-03Users shall view only the applicant fields required for their role.
PR-04Reviewer scores from other users shall remain hidden until authorized senior stages.
PR-05Administrative actions affecting roles or permissions shall be fully audited.

7. Security, POPIA, and Operations

7.1 POPIA and Security Requirements

IDRequirement
SR-01All personal data shall be encrypted in transit and at rest.
SR-02The system shall maintain an immutable audit trail of access and changes.
SR-03The system shall support applicant data export requests.
SR-04The system shall support deletion or anonymization workflows after the application cycle, subject to retention policy.
SR-05The system shall support breach response and notification procedures.
SR-06Passwords shall be stored using secure hashing such as Argon2id or bcrypt.
SR-07Sessions shall expire after inactivity and shall be rotated on sensitive events.
SR-08The application shall be delivered over HTTPS only.

7.2 Audit Logging

Audit records shall capture, at minimum:

  • actor user ID and active role
  • target entity or application ID
  • action performed
  • timestamp in UTC
  • before and after change summary where applicable
  • IP address, user agent, and correlation ID

8. Non-Functional Requirements

CategoryRequirement
AvailabilityThe platform should be available throughout the application cycle with planned maintenance windows communicated in advance.
PerformanceNormal page loads should remain responsive under expected seasonal usage and staff review operations.
ScalabilityThe solution should scale to support increased applicant volume without redesign of the workflow model.
MaintainabilityThe codebase shall use a layered architecture and version-controlled migrations.
ObservabilityThe system shall emit structured logs, alerts, and job status information for support and incident response.

9. Suggested Technical Stack

Application Stack

  • Frontend: Next.js or a .NET-aligned web front end
  • Backend: ASP.NET Core Web API on .NET 8
  • Database: PostgreSQL
  • Background processing: hosted services or job runner

AWS Services

  • CloudFront and WAF
  • App Runner or ECS Fargate
  • RDS PostgreSQL
  • S3, KMS, SES, CloudWatch
