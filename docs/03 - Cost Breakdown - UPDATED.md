## 3. Operations and Support

### Maintenance Fee: R4,500 / month (Incl. VAT)
This fixed monthly fee covers:
* Continuous monitoring and uptime checks.
* Security patches and framework updates.
* General technical support for staff users during the active cycle.
* **Bug fixes:** Developer-related issues are handled at no additional cost.

### Hosting and Infrastructure
Hosting is billed based on actual cloud consumption. Typical drivers are:
* Application traffic during peak periods (500+ applicants expected).
* Document storage and downloads.
* Database sizing and backup retention.
* Availability expectations (single-zone vs high availability).
