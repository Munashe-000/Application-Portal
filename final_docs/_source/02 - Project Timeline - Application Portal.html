1) Schedule Overview

Applications typically open mid-September. Admin training and the final technical handover should happen before that opening window.

Given the multi-role, multi-stage workflow and POPIA technical controls required from day one, a realistic plan is 10 to 12 weeks with three experienced developers working in parallel streams.

Phase Timeline Primary Goal
Phase 1: Setup & Alignment Weeks 1 - 2 Environment provisioning, workflow mapping, architecture lock.
Phase 2: Core Build Weeks 3 - 8 Applicant portal + staff workflows built in parallel.
Phase 3: Compliance & Hardening Weeks 9 - 10 Audit logging, exports/deletion, security review, client UAT.
Phase 4: Handover & Training Weeks 11 - 12 Production deployment, documentation, and admin/staff training.
